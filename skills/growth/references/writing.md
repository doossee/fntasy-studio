# Writing rules

Applies to everything: findings, plans, posts, captions, talks. Three words: **specifics, numbers, verbs.**

## Test every sentence

1. Can a competitor say the same sentence about themselves? Then it says nothing. Replace it with a fact only you can state.
2. Can a word be deleted without loss? Delete it.
3. Is there a number, a date, a name of a thing or an action? If none, the sentence is decoration.

## Words to cut

- **ru:** революционный, инновационный, уникальный, современный, качественный, эффективный, лучший, в современном мире, на сегодняшний день, не секрет что, является, осуществляет, в рамках, данный, широкий спектр, индивидуальный подход, команда профессионалов
- **uz:** zamonaviy, innovatsion, noyob, eng yaxshi, sifatli, samarali, zamonaviy dunyoda, bugungi kunda, hech kimga sir emaski, keng imkoniyatlar, professional jamoa, individual yondashuv
- **en:** revolutionary, innovative, unique, cutting-edge, seamless, game-changing, world-class, in today's world, unlock, elevate, leverage, empower, solutions, journey

A word from the list is allowed only with proof in the same sentence ("the only school in the city with …" + the source).

## Form

- One idea per post, one call to action, one link (with UTM).
- First line carries the fact. No warm-up, no question to "hook" unless the answer follows at once.
- Verbs in active voice: "мы проверяем домашние задания за сутки", not "проверка осуществляется".
- No exclamation mark in a row, at most one per post. At most one emoji, and only if the brand uses them.
- No hashtag walls: 0–3, and only ones people search.
- Prices, dates and times in full: `450 000 soʻm`, `6-dekabr, 11:00`.
- Numbers come from the profile or a **seen** finding. Unknown number → `[N]` and a note what to measure. Never invent results, reviews or student names.
- A claim about a competitor needs a link, and a neutral tone.

## Formats

| Format | Length | Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Telegram post | 300–700 characters | fact → detail → action with link |
| Carousel | 5–10 slides, ≤ 15 words each | cover with the promise → one point per slide → action |
| Story | ≤ 12 words on screen | one fact + link sticker |
| Reel | 15–30 s, 4–6 scenes, ≤ 8 words per scene | first 2 seconds state the benefit → proof → action |
| Caption for a reel or carousel | 1–3 sentences | what it is + where the link is |

## Two languages

Write each language from the facts, not from the other text. Keep numbers identical. Uzbek Latin:
oʻ, gʻ with ʻ (U+02BB). After writing, list for the user the words a native speaker should check
(terms, loanwords, the name of the offer).

## Last check

Read the text as the buyer on a phone: what do I get, how much, when, what do I press.
If one of the four is missing, add it or cut the post.
