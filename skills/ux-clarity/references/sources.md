# Sources

Every rule of the skill refers to a key from these tables. The pages were opened and checked when the skill was written; the wording of the rules is our own, source texts are not carried over. The "What it supports" column is our summary of a finding or a number, not a quotation. Where one source disagrees with another, the rule says which threshold the skill chose and why.

All sources are public web pages. Rights to them stay with their authors and publishers; follow the link for the original text.

## Mass-market apps

| Key | What it supports | Link |
| --- | --- | --- |
| APP-UNDO | A mainstream messenger lets the user undo deleting a chat within 5 seconds | Observed behaviour, announced in the vendor's release notes; no link kept, verify in the messenger your audience uses |

A bottom bar of five labelled sections, one round main-action button, a list of "recent recipients" and a large amount field are observed habits of mass-market messenger, social, banking and payment apps (the apps your users already use every day: profile → Product → familiar apps). There are no official pages describing those screens, so the rules call them "a habit of the audience" and back them with LAW-JAKOB, NNG-MOBILE-NAV and AND-NAV, not with a link to an app.

## Numbers that did not make it into the rules

- The "three-click rule" is not confirmed (NNG-3CLICK). The skill counts taps to the start of a daily action as its own norm, not as a law.
- "7 ± 2" is about short-term memory, not about the number of menu items. Not used.
- The "thumb zone" as a coloured map: the author of the data himself considers the picture wrong (HOOBER-TOUCH). Only the conclusion is used: do not put important things in the top corners.
- A strip display time of "4–10 seconds" is the wording of an old Material version; it was not found on the current pages. The 5-second threshold is taken from APP-UNDO and is the skill's own norm.
- Baymard percentages were obtained on online shops in the USA and Europe: take the direction, not the number.
- The touch target differs between sources: 24 px (WCAG AA), 44 px (WCAG AAA, Apple), 48 dp (Android), 7 mm (Baymard), 1 cm (NN/g). In this skill: 44 px is the norm (profile: `touch-target`), 24 px is the hard minimum.
- A "reading age of 9" was not found on the current GOV.UK pages. The skill uses sentence length (GOV-PLAIN, NNG-PLAIN) and stop words.
