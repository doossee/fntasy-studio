# The template look

Screens made quickly — by people under deadline or by generators — drift towards the same look. It is not wrong, but it says "made from a kit", and the person learns nothing about this product from it. Before handing over, check for these signs. Each comes with what to do instead.

| Sign | Why it reads as template | Instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Every block in a white card with the same shadow and radius | Equal frames say everything is equally important | Group with spacing; use a frame only where a thing is truly separate; vary size by importance |
| A large gradient across the hero or header | Decoration with no meaning, often purple-to-blue | Brand colour as a flat area with a job, or a real image of the product's world |
| Everything centred | Centre alignment hides structure and makes scanning slower | Start-aligned text on shared edges; centre only short, single statements |
| Three feature columns with an icon in a circle on top | The most common landing-page block | Show the product doing the thing: a real screen, a real number, a short story |
| Emoji as icons | Inconsistent size, colour and meaning across platforms | One icon set (`system.md`) |
| Grey text on grey cards, all at 14 px | Even and dull; often fails contrast | Three levels of text with size, weight and colour; measure contrast |
| Stat tiles: "1,234 Users ↑12%" × 4 | Numbers without a question | Lead with the one number that answers the screen's question; put the others in context |
| Generic stock-style illustration of people with laptops | Says nothing about this product | No illustration, or one in the brand's style with a job |
| Badges and pills on everything | Emphasis everywhere is emphasis nowhere | Status only where the state matters for the task |
| Placeholder copy ("Lorem ipsum", "Welcome to your dashboard!", "Unlock your potential") | Empty words fill the space a real message should use | Write the real message: what is here and what to do |
| Dark theme as pure black with neon accents | Glare and vibrating colours | Designed dark palette (`ui-rules` COLOR-4) |
| Glass, blur and glow added for depth | Effects with no job; often fail contrast | One depth strategy (`system.md`) |
| Identical spacing everywhere (16 px between everything) | No grouping, no rhythm | Spacing scale with clear inside/between steps |

None of these is forbidden on its own; a centred hero or a card grid can be the right call. The problem is reaching for them by default. If you keep one, say why in the hand-over.
